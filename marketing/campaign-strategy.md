# Sophia Martin Jewelry — 7-Day Instagram Launch Campaign

**Website:** https://sophiamartinjewelry.com
**Campaign window:** 7 consecutive days (one post/day)
**Objective:** Drive profile visits → website clicks → first purchases, while
building a recognizable brand voice from day one.

## 1. Campaign goals

| Goal | Metric | 7-day target (adjust to your baseline) |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Reach / impressions | Grow reach vs. prior week |
| Engagement | Saves + shares + comments | Saves are the priority signal |
| Traffic | Link clicks (bio link / link sticker) | Clicks to product pages |
| Conversion | Add-to-cart, checkouts w/ launch code | First sales from the code `[LAUNCH10]` |
| Audience | New followers | Steady daily net-positive |

**Why saves and shares over likes:** Instagram's ranking rewards content
people send to a friend or save for later. Jewelry is a considered,
gift-driven purchase, so "send this to someone who'd love it" and
"save for your wishlist" CTAs do more work than "double-tap."

## 2. Target audience

- **Primary:** Women 25–45, interested in everyday luxury, demi-fine and
  fine jewelry, minimal/elevated style, self-gifting and milestone gifting
  (anniversaries, birthdays, "treat yourself").
- **Secondary:** Gift-buyers (partners, friends) looking for a thoughtful,
  giftable piece in the `[$ PRICE RANGE]` range.
- **Mindset:** Values craftsmanship, materials they can trust (e.g.
  `[14k gold / gold vermeil / sterling silver / lab-grown stones]`), and a
  brand with a point of view — not fast-fashion costume jewelry.

## 3. Positioning & brand voice

- **Positioning statement (edit to taste):** *Sophia Martin Jewelry makes
  elevated everyday pieces designed to be worn, layered, and lived in — fine
  craftsmanship without the fine-jewelry intimidation.*
- **Voice:** Warm, confident, a little intimate. Talks to one person, not a
  crowd. Specific about materials and craft. Never hypey or discount-screamy.
- **Three words:** *elevated · personal · enduring.*
- **Visual style:** Consistent light, warm neutral backgrounds, skin-on-metal
  close-ups, natural daylight. Pick 2–3 brand colors and reuse them so the
  grid reads as one brand.

## 4. Hashtag strategy

Instagram favors a **small, relevant** set over 30 generic tags. Use
**8–12 per post**, mixing three tiers so you're not only competing in
giant hashtags:

- **Broad (reach, high competition):** `#finejewelry` `#jewelrylover`
  `#everydayjewelry`
- **Niche (your lane):** `#demifine` `#goldjewelry` `#layerednecklaces`
  `#jewelrydesigner` `#handmadejewelry`
- **Branded / community (own these):** `#SophiaMartinJewelry`
  `#WornBySophiaMartin` `#[YOUR BRANDED TAG]`

Keep the branded tags on **every** post so UGC and reposts accumulate under
them. Rotate the broad/niche tags so posts don't look spammy-identical.
Put hashtags in the **first comment or end of caption** — both work; pick one
and stay consistent.

## 5. Posting cadence & timing

- **1 post/day for 7 days.** Mix formats: Reels (reach), carousels (saves),
  single images (simple + clean).
- **Best-guess posting times** (verify in your own Instagram Insights once you
  have data — your audience's active hours win over any generic advice):
  - Weekdays: **11:00–13:00** or **18:00–20:00** local time.
  - Weekend: **10:00–12:00** local time.
- **Always** add a Stories post the same day pointing to the feed post
  (use the Link sticker to the product page) — Stories drive the clicks.

## 6. Content pillars (what every post should be one of)

1. **Hero/Product** — the piece, shot beautifully, with the "why it's special."
2. **Craft/Materials** — proof of quality (metal, stones, how it's made).
3. **Styling/How-to-wear** — layering, stacking, day-to-night.
4. **Story/Founder** — who Sophia Martin is, the brand's reason to exist.
5. **Social proof/UGC** — reviews, customers wearing it, press.
6. **Offer/CTA** — the launch incentive, clearly.

The 7-day calendar below hits every pillar at least once.

## 7. The launch offer (edit before posting)

- **Code:** `[LAUNCH10]` — `[10% off first order / free shipping over $X]`
- **Valid:** first `[7 days]` of the campaign only (urgency).
- Make sure the code is live in your store checkout **before** Day 1 posts.

## 8. Measurement & iteration

- Check Instagram **Insights** daily: reach, saves, shares, profile visits,
  link clicks per post.
- After Day 3, **double down** on whichever format/pillar is getting the most
  saves and clicks — repeat its structure for the remaining days.
- Track sales against the launch code to tie revenue back to the campaign.

## 9. Compliance notes

- Instagram's terms require posting **content you own or have rights to.**
  Only use product photos/UGC you have permission to use.
- If you ever gift product in exchange for posts, creators must disclose it
  (`#ad`/paid partnership label). Keep the brand's own posts honest — no fake
  reviews or fabricated scarcity.
