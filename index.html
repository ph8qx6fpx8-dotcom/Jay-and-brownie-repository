Caveats
  • One Ridge regression per artist; outliers and macro shocks are smoothed out.
  • The 10-year projection extrapolates the artist's in-sample trend plus your market-drift slider; confidence widens with horizon.
  • Categorical levels not seen in training (a brand-new auction house, etc.) fall back to a baseline.
  • Prices should be hammer + buyer's premium in USD. If your CSV uses hammer-only, gross it up first.

How to read this
  • "Model value" is what the regression expects the work to fetch at auction on the valuation date under the chosen scenario — not a guaranteed sale price. It needs ≥6 past sales for that artist in the loaded data.
  • "Your estimate" is the low/mid/high you supplied in the CSV (e.g. from your own spreadsheet). It is shown as-is and is the only figure for works the model cannot value.
  • The portfolio range sums each work's low and high bounds. That is a conservative envelope: it assumes every work lands at its low (or high) at once.
  • Gains compare "value now" to acquired_price_usd and ignore buyer's/seller's premiums, taxes, and holding costs.
  • Works by artists with fewer than 6 sales in the loaded data are listed but not valued.
