## 1. Executive Summary & 90-Day Objectives

Sophia Martin Jewelry sells one-of-a-kind vintage and Art Deco-revival **fine** jewelry ($300–$3,500+). The product is inseparable from its story: Sophia Lapat, a lawyer's daughter who trailed her father Martin through Americana antique shows and talked him into a silver heart pendant, now reimagines antique and Deco pieces into wearable heirlooms. That is the entire strategic advantage. We do not compete on price, speed, or trend — we compete on **history, craftsmanship, scarcity, and storytelling**. Every post should feel like being handed a loupe and a story, not a sale.

Because inventory is genuinely one-of-a-kind, we have an honest, luxury-appropriate scarcity mechanism — "when it's gone, it's gone" — that we use as a truth, never as a fake-urgency gimmick.

The 90-day job is to **build a qualified, aesthetically-aligned following that saves and shares** (the behaviors that signal collector intent and feed the algorithm), **drive that audience to the site and email list**, and **convert a small number of high-value sales** — recognizing that at a $300–$3,500 price point, a handful of sales is a real revenue outcome.

### Primary objectives

| # | Objective | Why it matters here |
|---|-----------|---------------------|
| 1 | **Awareness** — grow a *qualified* audience of vintage/Deco lovers | Reach the right women, not the most people |
| 2 | **Engagement** — maximize saves & shares | Saves signal "I want this / I'll return to this" — the collector tell |
| 3 | **Traffic** — move people to product pages & email capture | Instagram/TikTok don't sell well in-app for fine jewelry; the site does |
| 4 | **Sales** — convert considered, high-AOV purchases | Few sales, high value; nurture-driven not impulse |

### KPIs to track (prioritized)

**Set every target against the brand's own current baseline — do not chase absolute numbers.** In Week 1, record baselines for each metric below; then set a 30/60/90 growth target (sensible starting ambitions in parentheses).

- **Saves per post** *(primary engagement KPI)* — target a rising saves-per-reach rate (e.g., +25% by day 90)
- **Shares/sends per post** *(primary virality KPI)* — the second-strongest collector/gift signal
- **Profile visits** — intent to learn more about the brand
- **Link clicks** (bio link + Stories link stickers + Pinterest outbound) — the traffic KPI
- **Email signups from social** — the owned-audience KPI (most important for fine-jewelry conversion)
- **Revenue & revenue attributable to social** (discount-code-free; use UTM links + "how did you hear about us")
- Supporting: follower *quality* growth (not just count), reach vs. follower ratio, Reels plays, Pinterest outbound clicks & saves, Stories completion/reply rate, DM product inquiries

> **Note on sales targets:** at this AOV, even 3–8 pieces/month is meaningful. Set a dollar target, not a unit target, and track **assisted** revenue (social → email → site), because the path is long and considered.

## 2. Audience Personas

### Persona A — "Eleanor, the Collector" (age 52)
- **Who:** Attorney/partner or business owner, high disposable income, owns vintage jewelry already, reads about Art Deco, watches auction/estate content.
- **Motivations:** Provenance and authenticity; owning a piece *no one else has*; building a small personal collection; self-reward for milestones.
- **Objections:** "Is it authentic / what era / what are the materials?" "Can I trust fine jewelry bought online?" "Is $2,000 fair for this piece?"
- **Where online:** Instagram (saves heavily), Pinterest boards ("Art Deco jewelry," "estate jewelry"), 1stDibs-adjacent content, antique-show and auction-house accounts, Substack/long-form.
- **How we win her:** Education, close-up craftsmanship, era/material specifics, the Martin story. She converts after she trusts.

### Persona B — "Claire, the Milestone Gifter" (age 44)
- **Who:** Professional with family; buying for a 10th/25th anniversary, 40th/50th birthday, or a legacy gift for a daughter/mother.
- **Motivations:** A gift with *meaning* and permanence; something that becomes a family heirloom; a story she can tell when she gives it.
- **Objections:** "Will she love it / is it 'her'?" "Is this too much / not enough?" "What if I pick wrong on a one-of-a-kind item (no second chance)?"
- **Where online:** Instagram (Reels + Stories), Pinterest ("anniversary gift for her," "heirloom jewelry"), gift-guide and wedding-adjacent content.
- **How we win her:** Gifting narratives, "the heirloom you pass down," styling for the recipient, concierge-feeling DMs, scarcity framed as *this exact piece will not come again*.

### Persona C — "Maya, the Design-Led Self-Purchaser" (age 34)
- **Who:** Creative-professional income, loves Deco/vintage aesthetics (interiors, fashion, film), aspirational collector starting at the accessible end.
- **Motivations:** A statement piece that expresses taste; distinctiveness; the *story* as social currency.
- **Objections:** Price sensitivity at the top of the range; "where would I wear a lariat?"; wants reassurance it's wearable, not just beautiful.
- **Where online:** TikTok + Instagram Reels (discovery), Pinterest (planning), saves aspirationally for later.
- **How we win her:** Entry pieces (the $300 Crystal & Green Glass Pendant), modern styling, "how to wear a fringe pendant to dinner," behind-the-scenes storytelling that makes the brand feel like a world she wants in.

## 3. Brand Positioning & Messaging

**Positioning statement:** *For women who see jewelry as history worn close to the skin, Sophia Martin Jewelry reimagines one-of-a-kind vintage and Art Deco pieces into fine heirlooms — each with a past, a maker's hand, and no second copy.*

### Messaging pillars
1. **History you can wear** — real eras, real provenance, the Art Deco world.
2. **One-of-a-kind, truthfully** — genuine scarcity; when it's gone, it's gone.
3. **Reimagined, not merely restored** — like Martin, we don't just collect; we make new works of art.
4. **Heirloom permanence** — made to be passed down; the opposite of fast fashion.
5. **The Martin story** — a father, a daughter, a silver heart pendant, a legacy carried forward in a brown bow.

### Voice: Do / Don't

**Do**
- Lead with story, era, and material specifics ("a 1920s-spirit onyx & ruby fringe").
- Speak warmly and personally — this is a family brand with a name attached.
- Use scarcity as *fact*: "one piece exists."
- Let the object breathe — fewer words, better words.
- Invite the collector: "save this," "which era speaks to you?"

**Don't**
- Never say "sale," "discount," "deal," "% off," "limited time only!!!" or use urgency emojis/countdowns as pressure.
- No exclamation-heavy, hype-y caption copy.
- Don't call pieces "everyday basics," "demi-fine," "affordable," or "trendy."
- Don't over-hashtag the caption into clutter.
- Don't make claims about an era/material you can't substantiate — authenticity is the whole product.

## 4. Channel Strategy

### Instagram — PRIMARY
- **Role:** Home base for brand world, product reveals, story, and community. Where collectors and gifters validate trust before buying.
- **Content types:** Reels (reach/discovery — new-arrival reveals, styling, Sophia-to-camera story), carousels (the deepest-saved format — era education, "the story behind the piece," materials breakdowns), Stories (daily presence, polls, link stickers, DMs/concierge, new-arrival "just listed"), occasional static for hero imagery.
- **Format mix (weekly):** ~3–4 Reels, 2 carousels, 1 static, daily Stories (5–7 days).
- **Cadence:** 5–6 feed posts/week + daily Stories.
- **Why it fits:** Visual-first, strong saves/shares mechanics, Story link stickers drive site traffic, DMs enable concierge selling for one-of-a-kind, high-consideration pieces.

### Pinterest — STRONG SECONDARY (high purchase intent)
- **Role:** Evergreen discovery + search engine for exactly our aesthetic ("Art Deco jewelry," "vintage statement pendant," "heirloom gift," "estate jewelry"). Long content half-life; users are in *planning/buying* mode.
- **Content types:** Vertical product Pins (every product = a Pin linking to its product page), carousel/idea Pins (styling, era guides), themed boards: *Art Deco Revival*, *One-of-a-Kind Pendants*, *Fringe & Lariats*, *Heirloom Gifts*, *The History of Deco Jewelry*, *Blush, Brown & Cream* (brand world).
- **Format mix:** Standard Pins (every piece, re-pinned across relevant boards) + 2–3 idea Pins/week.
- **Cadence:** 5–10 fresh/repurposed Pins per week (repurpose Instagram assets into vertical Pins).
- **Why it fits:** Pinterest's user base skews women 30–60 with buying intent and loves vintage/Deco — a near-perfect demographic and aesthetic match, with SEO-driven *free* long-tail traffic to product pages.

### TikTok — STORYTELLING / DISCOVERY
- **Role:** Reach new audiences through narrative and behind-the-scenes; humanize the brand via Sophia and the Martin story.
- **Content types:** Sourcing/antique-show storytelling, "the history of this piece," reimagining process, era mini-lessons, "fine jewelry you've never seen before," the founder origin story (silver heart pendant).
- **Format mix:** 3–4 short videos/week; voiceover + close-ups; story-driven hooks.
- **Cadence:** 3–4/week (repurpose best as Instagram Reels and vice versa).
- **Why it fits:** TikTok rewards story and authenticity over polish; our deepest asset (a genuine, emotional founder story and real antiques) is exactly what performs. Use it for top-of-funnel; convert via profile link → email.

### Email Capture (owned audience — note)
Social is rented; email is owned, and for fine jewelry the sale usually closes off-platform after nurturing. Make **email signup a cross-channel CTA**:
- Bio link → a simple landing page offering **"First Look" early access to new one-of-a-kind arrivals** (not a discount — early access fits the luxury/scarcity model perfectly).
- Stories link stickers and Pinterest Pins point to the same capture.
- Capture also powers the honest scarcity loop: subscribers hear about a piece first *because* there's only one.

## 7. Organic Growth Tactics

- **Hashtags (IG):** 8–15, in the first comment or lightly in-caption, mixing niche + mid + branded: `#ArtDecoJewelry #VintageJewelry #EstateJewelry #OneOfAKindJewelry #FineJewelry #HeirloomJewelry #StatementPendant #AntiqueJewelry #DecoRevival #WornBySophiaMartin`. Research and rotate; avoid giant generic tags where we'd be buried.
- **Pinterest SEO:** Keyword-rich Pin titles/descriptions and board names using buyer language ("Art Deco onyx & ruby fringe pendant," "one-of-a-kind heirloom anniversary gift"). Every product gets a Pin linking to its product page; refresh Pins and re-pin top performers.
- **Reels/TikTok hooks (first 1–2 seconds):** "This piece is over a hundred years old…"; "There's only one of these in the world."; "My dad taught me to reimagine antiques…"; "Fine jewelry you've never seen before."; "Don't buy a lariat until you watch this."
- **Saves-driven CTAs:** "Save this for your collection." · "Save so you can find it again — there's only one." · "Save this era guide." Saves are our north-star engagement metric.
- **Shares-driven CTAs:** "Send this to the person who should own it." · "Share with whoever's hunting for an heirloom gift."
- **UGC program — #WornBySophiaMartin:** invite every customer (card in packaging + follow-up email) to post wearing their piece and tag the hashtag; repost with their story. Builds trust where it's scarcest — buying fine jewelry online.
- **Collaborations (organic, aesthetic-matched):** vintage-fashion and interiors micro-creators, Art Deco/antiques educators, estate-jewelry accounts, and "old money"/vintage-aesthetic creators — prioritize fit and audience quality over size; offer story-driven features or gifting of accessible pieces in exchange for authentic content.
- **Concierge DMs:** treat product inquiries as high-touch sales conversations; answer with era/material detail and gentle scarcity, route to product page or booking a call.
- **Cross-pollinate:** repurpose every strong Reel into a TikTok and a vertical Pin; every carousel becomes a Pinterest idea Pin.

## 8. Tools & Workflow

- **Scheduling/publishing:** one scheduler across channels (e.g., Later, Planoly, or Metricool) — all three skew visual and support IG + Pinterest + TikTok with a content calendar.
- **Pinterest:** native Pinterest + scheduler for consistent fresh Pins; use Pinterest Trends for keyword research.
- **Design:** Canva for carousels/Pins/templates to keep the blush/brown/cream, bow, antique-wallpaper world consistent.
- **Analytics:** native insights (IG, Pinterest, TikTok) + Metricool/Later dashboards; **UTM links** on every outbound link for site attribution; site/GA for traffic→email→revenue.
- **Asset library:** organized folder of product photos/videos per SKU (close-ups, on-model, era context) so each piece can be cut into Reel + carousel + Story + Pin.
- **Content bank:** batch-produce 2–4 weeks ahead; keep an evergreen education/story backlog so new one-of-a-kind arrivals can slot in immediately.

### Measurement cadence
- **Weekly (30 min):** top posts by saves & shares, profile visits, link clicks, email signups, new DMs/inquiries. Note what format/pillar won; double down next week.
- **Monthly (90 min):** full KPI review vs. baseline and 30/60/90 targets; follower *quality*; Pinterest outbound clicks; social-assisted revenue; retire underperforming pillars/formats; reset next month's content themes.
