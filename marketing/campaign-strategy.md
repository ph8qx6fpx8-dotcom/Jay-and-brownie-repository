## 1. Campaign goals

| Goal | Metric | 7-day target (adjust to your baseline) |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Reach / impressions | Grow reach vs. prior week |
| Engagement | Saves + shares + comments | Saves are the priority signal |
| Traffic | Link clicks (bio link / link sticker) | Clicks to individual product pages |
| Conversion | Add-to-cart, checkouts | First sales of one-of-a-kind pieces |
| Audience | New followers | Steady daily net-positive |

**Why saves and shares over likes:** Instagram's ranking rewards content
people send to a friend or save for later. Fine vintage jewelry is a
considered, often gift-driven purchase, so "send this to someone who'd love
it" and "save before it's gone" CTAs do more work than "double-tap." Because
every piece is one-of-a-kind, a save also signals genuine intent on a specific
item — treat it as a near-cart signal.

## 2. Target audience

- **Primary:** Women ~30–60 with disposable income who love vintage, Art Deco,
  and antique aesthetics — collectors, and those buying heirlooms for
  themselves.
- **Secondary:** Thoughtful gift-buyers (partners, family, close friends)
  seeking a meaningful, one-of-a-kind piece for an anniversary or milestone,
  roughly in the `$300–$3,500` range of the current collection.
- **Mindset:** Values history, craftsmanship, and provenance; drawn to pieces
  with a story and a point of view — not mass-produced or fast-fashion
  jewelry. Understands that one-of-a-kind means now or never.

## 3. Positioning & brand voice

- **Positioning statement:** *Sophia Martin Jewelry sources one-of-a-kind
  vintage and Art Deco fine jewelry and reimagines each piece into a wearable
  heirloom — history kept, beauty renewed, and passed down anew.*
- **Founder story (use often):** Named for founder Sophia Lapat's father,
  Martin — a lawyer by day, passionate about Americana antiques, who
  reimagined them into new works of art. Sophia tagged along to fine antique
  shows as a child, was drawn to the trays of vintage jewelry, and the simple
  silver heart pendant her father bought her sparked a lifelong love.
- **Voice:** Elegant, warm, heritage-driven, storytelling. Talks to one person.
  Emphasizes history, one-of-a-kind, reimagined, heirloom. **Never hypey or
  discount-screamy.**
- **Four words:** *elegant · heirloom · one-of-a-kind · reimagined.*
- **Visual style:** The delicate brown bow logo; a blush/brown/cream palette;
  an Art Deco and vintage visual world — antique wallpaper, taper candles,
  bows. Warm, candlelit light; macro detail on diamonds, onyx, pearls, and
  gold. Reuse these so the grid reads as one refined brand.

## 5. Hashtag strategy

Instagram favors a **small, relevant** set over 30 generic tags. Use
**8–12 per post**, mixing three tiers so you're not only competing in giant
hashtags:

- **Broad (reach, high competition):** `#finejewelry` `#vintagejewelry`
  `#artdeco`
- **Niche (your lane):** `#artdecojewelry` `#antiquejewelry` `#heirloomjewelry`
  `#estatejewelry` `#oneofakind`
- **Branded / community (own these):** `#SophiaMartinJewelry`
  `#WornBySophiaMartin`

Keep the branded tags on **every** post so UGC and reposts accumulate under
them. Rotate the broad/niche tags so posts don't look spammy-identical. Put
hashtags in the **first comment or end of caption** — both work; pick one and
stay consistent.

## 6. Posting cadence & timing

- **1 post/day for 7 days.** Mix formats: Reels (reach), carousels (saves and
  storytelling across detail/history slides), single images (simple + clean).
- **Best-guess posting times** (verify in your own Instagram Insights once you
  have data — your audience's active hours win over any generic advice):
  - Weekdays: **11:00–13:00** or **18:00–20:00** local time.
  - Weekend: **10:00–12:00** local time.
- **Always** add a Stories post the same day pointing to the feed post (use the
  Link sticker to the exact product page) — Stories drive the clicks.

## 7. Content pillars (what every post should be one of)

1. **Brand/Launch** — "Heirlooms Redefined" and the founder-father story.
2. **Hero/Product** — a specific one-of-a-kind piece, shot beautifully, with
   its story and "why it's special."
3. **Craft/History** — Art Deco design, provenance, and reimagining proof.
4. **Styling/How-to-wear** — styling a statement heirloom for modern life.
5. **Story/Founder** — Sophia and her father Martin; social proof / UGC.
6. **Gifting** — the heirloom as a meaningful gift (keepsake packaging).
7. **Scarcity/CTA** — one-of-a-kind, never restocked, when it's gone it's gone.

The 7-day calendar hits every pillar at least once, in a deliberate arc from
brand introduction to scarcity-driven last call.

## 8. The launch incentive (elegant, not discount-driven)

Because every piece is one-of-a-kind, **scarcity is the real lever — not
price cuts.** Lead with *"when it's gone, it's gone"* and offer tasteful,
luxury-tone perks rather than a loud promo:

- **Complimentary keepsake packaging** on every order.
- **Free insured shipping.**
- **A personal, handwritten note from Sophia.**

If you want a time-bound incentive, keep it soft and framed as a launch-week
courtesy. `[OPTIONAL: a tasteful launch code — e.g. a small thank-you toward a
first order — only if it fits the brand; make sure it is live in checkout
before Day 1.]` Do not fabricate urgency; the scarcity here is genuine.

## 9. Measurement & iteration

- Check Instagram **Insights** daily: reach, saves, shares, profile visits,
  link clicks per post (and which specific piece drives them).
- After Day 3, **double down** on whichever format/pillar is earning the most
  saves and clicks — repeat its structure for the remaining days.
- Track sales against pieces sold; as one-of-a-kind items sell, update Stories
  with tasteful "found its person" notes to reinforce real scarcity.

## 10. Compliance notes

- Instagram's terms require posting **content you own or have rights to.** Only
  use product photos/UGC you have permission to use.
- If you ever gift product in exchange for posts, creators must disclose it
  (`#ad`/paid partnership label). Keep the brand's own posts honest — **no fake
  reviews or fabricated scarcity.** With one-of-a-kind pieces, the scarcity is
  real; never manufacture it, and only mark a piece sold when it truly is.
